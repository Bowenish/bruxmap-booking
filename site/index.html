Before you book: please check that you meet the study requirements

  • You are 18 years or older.
  • You understand English well enough to follow written and spoken instructions (the session is run in English; the information sheet and consent form also exist in Danish).
  • You have your own natural teeth and no removable dentures, and in your own judgement you can chew and clench firmly without discomfort.
  • You do not currently have jaw pain, a temporomandibular (jaw-joint) disorder, limited mouth opening or other problems of the jaw or chewing muscles, and you are not in orthodontic treatment (braces).
  • You are not prone to skin irritation and have no known allergy to adhesive electrodes or electrode gel.
  • Nothing prevents you from performing the jaw movements in the study (chewing, clenching, drinking water).
  • The study measures bite force with a sensor you bite on. If you have dental implants, or your dentist has advised you not to bite hard, please do not take part.
